# Migration status

Target: Minecraft 26.3, Fabric Loader 0.19.5, Fabric API 0.162.0+26.3, Java 25. Upstream baseline: Ripples of the Past 0.2.2.2 at `72862a5826ed45d1dc26e90b37853097acda35de`.

The original project contains 1,273 Java files and 158,639 source lines, including 394 files importing Forge and 58 mixin classes. Draft 3 added uppercut, Star Finger, inhale, zoom, manual control, and regional time stop. The user accepted Drafts 2 and 3, reporting a stamina display concern in Draft 3. Draft 4 distinguishes unlimited Creative stamina, verifies Survival HUD pixels, and adds automatic finishers and combat movement restrictions. Draft 5 adds self-piercing Stand Arrows, original Beetle Arrow loot, persistent XP/virus acquisition and owner feedback. Draft 6 adds original Stand Resolve thresholds, combat gain/decay/records, unlocks, stat development, human Time Stop training and a progress screen. The user playtested Draft 6 and reported Stand camera obstruction in both views. Draft 7 adds first-person Stand/finger fading, a collision-aware rear shoulder camera with aim convergence, and hides the body during first-person manual control. It does not represent full parity with the original mod.

Draft 7 adds a fourth suite for local opacity, first-person eye position, shoulder placement, aimed blocks and entities, near-wall collision, front view, manual body hiding, dismissal and a new-world reset. The progression fixture now teleports through the connection so client and server agree on the test arena.

- Sixty-six mod server GameTests plus one framework test cover permissions, persistence, combat, special ability costs/range/walls/cooldowns, manual control input validation/range/expiry, time-stop behavior, automatic finisher thresholds/launch/remainder, movement factors and modifier cleanup.

- The time-stop test runs in a separate environment batch. It advances the real server and verifies caster immunity, mob/projectile/player clocks, frozen movement packet rejection, damage acceptance, cooldown progression, exhaustion and natural expiration. It checks that button/fluid scheduled ticks remain pending, furnaces pause, and all resume afterward.
- Twenty-four model checks verify original geometry, nine imported clips, dispatch, metadata and deterministic generation. The body remains 74 parts / 107 cubes; Star Finger uses a separate layer with the original 32×32 projectile texture and repeating segment dimensions.
- Survival HUD checks measure rendered pixel widths at full, half and empty stamina, during barrage drain and resting regeneration. Dedicated-server checks verify half-to-quarter changes. Creative mode switches remove the finite bar and display unlimited stamina. - Combat tests verify preservation of other speed modifiers, expiry, death, disconnect, dimension changes and manual body locking. Real-client checks cover movement attribute synchronization, blocked sprint input, restoration and charged heavy-key selection.
- Real-client coverage uses actual keys and packets for all six new abilities, zoom reset, manual camera movement/return, menu cancellation, time-stop charge/early resume, mob freezing, save/reload, and a dedicated-server connection.
- Additional S2C fixtures verify that a frozen local player cannot move or advance its entity clock, and a distant stop/resume cannot erase another region's stop. Dedicated-server disconnect during an active stop verifies manifestation cleanup.

Screenshots and Gradle reports are generated under `fabric/build/` and excluded from Git. Release verification uses `./gradlew build runClientGameTest -PacceptMinecraftEula=true` with Java 25. ## Deliberate draft limits

- Star Platinum can now be acquired in Survival via a Beetle Arrow from a desert-pyramid chest. Only Star Platinum is in the migrated pool. Survival unlocks Uppercut/Inhale/Star Finger/Time Stop at Resolve levels 1/2/3/4. The original customizable action-selection UI remains Phase 4 work.
- Resolve uses the original default thresholds, attack and low-health boosts, and the ten highest combat records. Accepted Stand damage earns full raw points; user attacks while summoned earn half. Passive animals, self-hits and invulnerable/rejected hits do not count. The meter decays after 400 ticks (twice as fast while dismissed); its value then falls by two per tick. Expiration records the peak even when an unsummoned two-tick countdown lands on zero, correcting the original missed-record edge case.
- Resolve effects advance at most one level at a time and retain the original drain/duration tables. Levels develop stats by one quarter. Resistance applies after absorption, including environmental damage except void; the health change is authoritative, but vanilla combat-stat accounting still records pre-Resolve damage. Temporary Resolve gives full 66.67% resistance, fourfold training and Star Finger's reduced additional cooldown. Effect particles/shader, souls, configurable progression, extra max-level counters, other-power multipliers and the original cosmetic chat/remote boost display remain pending.
- Time Stop starts at five ticks and trains by 0.25 point per actual stopped tick, up to 95 points/100 ticks. Training occurs once on resume, including partial stops; charging alone gives none. Cost scales as 225 upfront plus 900/duration per tick. Cooldown is three times elapsed ticks, divided by three during Resolve; Creative bypasses training duration, costs and active-stop cooldown. The 20-tick charge-cancel safety cooldown remains. Learned duration, levels and fractional training persist; combat Resolve resets on death while records remain. Creative access is a temporary bypass rather than permanent earned levels.
- K opens a scrollable, narrated, read-only progression overview with remappable key labels, mouse/keyboard scrolling and locked-action feedback. Server entry points enforce unlocks. Prior Fabric development maps to the nearest original Resolve level when an old save lacks the attachment; ownership remains. Existing Survival users may have moves relocked. Clear/regrant retains learned progress; the operator-only reset command clears it explicitly. Hamon is not part of this slice.
- Arrow self-piercing uses original default costs: 30 XP levels plus 5 per prior arrow awakening, one level every ten ticks, 0.75 paid damage or 7.5 unpaid damage, and a maximum duration of (required levels + 1) seconds. Paying the full cost or surviving that maximum duration grants the Stand; afterward, healing restores 0.25 health per tick until full. Milk preserves the virus. Death cancels the attempt and keeps history. Administrator effect removal cancels rather than awarding a Stand, deliberately preventing forced removal from bypassing acquisition.
- Normal/Beetle durability is 25/250. Original Beetle Arrow loot is an additional 2% roll on desert-pyramid tables, including datapack replacements as upstream. Existing opened chests are not refilled. Normal-arrow recipes await meteorite materials/world generation; bow/crossbow/dispenser shooting, arrow enchantments, mob acquisition and configurable Stand pools/costs remain unported. Creative bypasses XP, virus and durability loss.
- Time stop uses the original default square radius of twelve chunks around activation, a forty-tick charge and a hundred-tick human maximum. Overlapping regions are rejected. Vampire duration, time-stop invasion, instant blink, original shader effects, delayed damage accumulation and player visibility rules remain unported. Damage takes effect immediately; normal damage cooldowns advance while frozen.
- Scheduled block/fluid ticks are retained and run after resume. Random ticks and block entities pause. Immediate neighbor updates and events from outside the frozen region are not a full world snapshot and are not deferred. Caster interaction inside the region remains possible.
- Client stop state is tracked per caster, synchronized on join and dimension change, and cleared by authoritative resume/disconnect. Damage and momentum changes during a stop remain for resumed simulation. Cooldowns survive dismissal, death, regrant and reconnect within a server session; cooldown persistence across a server restart is not implemented.
- Manual control uses a held key, an anchored body, a ten-block sphere, server-clamped directional input, and a twelve-tick lease. The manifestation can phase through blocks, cannot load chunks, and uses its own attack origin. Original manual-control interaction modes and first-person Stand hands remain incomplete.
- Star Finger respects walls and hits each reachable living target once per action. Original block breaking and separately damageable body-part projectile behavior are not implemented. Inhale restores pull/air drain but uses vanilla cloud particles instead of the custom air-stream renderer.
- Uppercut unlocks at Resolve level 1, is available directly on B and automatically on V at half finisher charge, with launch and shield disable. The meter synchronizes to the client. Parry, barrage clashes, fist afterimages and automatic guarding remain incomplete. Movement factors use a synchronized speed attribute instead of the original client input scaling; full cross-mod movement compatibility is not claimed. Barrage timing remains deterministic rather than randomized.

Local builds live in `drafts/latest/` at the repository root; the previous draft is retained. GSD Core tracks requirements and phases under `.planning/`.
